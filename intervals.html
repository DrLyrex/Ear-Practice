Interval training tips

Intervals are the distance between two notes. If the full list feels overwhelming, begin with three strong anchors: major third, perfect fifth, and octave. Listen to whether the second note feels close, far, stable, bright, tense, or resolved.

Use ascending, descending, and harmonic modes to hear the same interval from different angles. Singing the sound before choosing an answer helps connect recognition with musicianship instead of pure guessing.
