Chord recognition tips

Chords are easier to recognize when you listen for their overall color before naming individual notes. Major chords often feel settled and bright, minor chords feel darker, and diminished or augmented chords create stronger tension.

Start with major and minor, then add suspended and seventh chords as your ear becomes more confident. Short, repeated sessions usually work better than long sessions with too many answer choices.
